# Data license and provenance

## Raw data

The raw hourly PM2.5 series (`data/raw/datos_lima_raw_mp2.5.csv`) were obtained
from the Servicio Nacional de Meteorología e Hidrología del Perú (SENAMHI),
Red de Monitoreo de Calidad del Aire (REMCA), <https://www.senamhi.gob.pe>.

The raw data remain subject to SENAMHI's terms of use. They are included here
to make the preprocessing audit reproducible. If the source terms do not allow
redistribution in your jurisdiction, remove `data/raw/` and obtain the file
from SENAMHI; the processed bases and audit files are sufficient to inspect
the analysis.

## Processed data and audit

The processed bases (`data/processed/`) and the row-level audit
(`data/audit/`) are derived works. To the extent permitted by the source terms,
they are released under CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-PAPER`). They contain no personal
data.
